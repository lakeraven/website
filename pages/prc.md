# Purchased/Referred Care (PRC) Software for Tribes

<p class="usa-intro">Corvid runs tribal PRC from referral to payment, alongside the EHR you already use.</p>

<p><span class="usa-tag">Early access</span></p>

Purchased/Referred Care (PRC), formerly Contract Health Services (CHS), pays for care that tribal and IHS clinics can't provide themselves. PRC staff check eligibility, rank requests by medical priority, commit funds from a fixed annual budget, and make sure outside providers are paid no more than Medicare-Like Rates. Too much of that work still happens on paper and spreadsheets.

Corvid is Lakeraven's referral management software, with PRC rules built in. It's open source and in early access.

---

## What Corvid does for PRC

- **Referrals to authorizations**  
  Track each referral from the clinic's request through review, approval or denial, and the authorization sent to the outside provider.

- **Eligibility checks**  
  Work through enrollment, residency, and alternate-resource checks, so PRC stays the payer of last resort. Each determination is recorded with an audit trail.

- **Medical priority**  
  Assign PRC medical priority levels, and route requests to committee review and management approval when your rules require it.

- **Budget obligations**  
  Check funds availability and obligate against your PRC budget before you approve care, so you can see what's committed and what's left.

- **Medicare-Like Rates repricing**  
  Reprice outside claims against CMS fee schedules for physician, outpatient, ambulatory surgery and inpatient hospital services.

- **Overpayment analysis**  
  Find past payments made above Medicare-Like Rates and build the report you need to recover them.

---

## Built to fit your program

- **Works with your EHR.** Corvid connects to any EHR through FHIR R4. You don't have to change EHRs to use it.
- **No patient charts stored.** Clinical and identifying data stays in your systems. Corvid holds the PRC workflow, not the chart.
- **Open source.** The code is public, so your program isn't locked in to one vendor.
- **Sold on its own.** Corvid doesn't require any other Lakeraven product.
