# Technology Modernization

<p class="usa-intro">Modern systems for public programs and private organizations</p>

Beyond tribal health, Lakeraven helps governments, healthcare organizations, and private companies modernize the systems their work runs on. We replace or rebuild legacy systems, move data to open platforms, build custom software and AI-assisted tools, and coach your team to run what we build together. Our approach is rooted in human-centered design, agile delivery, and cloud-native technology. Our goal is always the same: build what works and help teams sustain it.

---

## Who We Work With

- **Governments and public programs**  
  Tribal, state, and local governments and the programs they run, from benefits and eligibility to health and natural resources.

- **Healthcare organizations**  
  Independent clinics, community health centers, rural hospitals, and health systems that need their systems to talk to each other, their data out of legacy platforms, or a workflow their current vendor doesn’t cover.

- **Health plans and employer-sponsored plans**  
  Payers, self-funded employer plans, and the administrators who serve them, where claims, eligibility, and member data need cleaning up, connecting, or automating.

- **Companies and startups**  
  Private firms that need custom software, data pipelines, or AI-assisted tools built well and handed over in a state their own team can maintain. Our private-sector work includes AI-assisted document generation and insurance data processing on Snowflake.

---

## Our Approach

We partner with you to build modern services while strengthening your team’s long-term capacity.

- **Human-Centered Design**  
  We work side-by-side with users, staff, and leadership to design services that meet real needs. Our process includes inclusive research, journey mapping, and co-design.

- **Iterative Development**  
  We ship early and often, using agile practices and user feedback to improve services continuously and reduce delivery risk.

- **Cloud-Native Solutions**  
  We design scalable, secure platforms that reduce tech debt and operating costs, built to adapt as your mission or business evolves.

- **AI-Assisted Delivery, With People in Charge**  
  We use AI to speed up building and testing software, inside guardrails: automated checks are the hard floor no AI agent can bypass, models add a second review, and people decide anything about design or compliance.

## What We Do

### Digital Services and Software

- **UX Research & Strategy**  
  Service blueprints, journey maps, personas, and co-design with the people who use the service.

- **Product & Agile Delivery**  
  Outcome-focused product leadership and delivery using agile methods.

- **Custom Software Development**  
  Secure, modern, and maintainable platforms built on open standards and open source.

- **Cloud Architecture & DevOps**  
  Resilient infrastructure with continuous deployment, monitoring, and scaling.

- **Data Modernization**  
  Data cleanup, integration, and transition from legacy systems to affordable, open platforms.

- **AI-Assisted Tools**  
  Practical AI built into your workflows, such as drafting documents, extracting data from files, and triaging requests, with rules making the decisions and people reviewing the results.

  Connecting clinical and administrative systems using standards like HL7 FHIR, so records and referrals move without re-keying.

## How We Engage

- **Discovery**: a short, fixed-scope engagement to understand the problem and recommend a path, whether or not that path includes us.
- **Build**: a project team, assembled for your work, that designs, builds, and hands over a working system.
- **Embed**: our people working inside your team to add capacity and leave skills behind.

We contract directly with private organizations and as a prime or subcontractor on public work.
